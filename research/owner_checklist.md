2. **Confirm ForecastEx (FORECASTX) contracts can be discovered, quoted and ordered in the IBKR paper account** (reqContractDetails plus a resting limit order, read-only otherwise). Blocks: Phase 1 (latency probe, paper quoting) and Phase 3 paper runs. If unsupported, a fallback plan is needed.
3. **IBKR commission and pass-through of the ForecastEx $0.01 fee.** ForecastEx charges $0.01 per contract to both Yes and No sides (fee schedule read). Confirm from IBKR statements or a readable pricing page whether IBKR adds a commission, waives or passes through the $0.01, and any NFA or regulatory line items. The IBKR pricing page claim (USD 0.00 commission plus $0.01 exchange fee) was seen only as an excerpt. Also confirm that an open-and-flatten round trip is charged as two executions. Why: affects order-router logic and fill prices. Blocks: Phase 2 order router and sim fill model.
