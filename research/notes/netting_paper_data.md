### Additional facts for the integrator (not table rows)

- Per-contract fee (goal b): the rulebook sets no dollar fee. Rule 308 gives ForecastEx sole power to set fees, Rule 311(h) says fees are in a schedule on the ForecastEx website, and Rule 603(b)(2)(iv) (p51) treats any collateral in excess of the settlement amount per Yes/No pair as a fee due ForecastEx; Rule 403(c) (p32) allows paired Bid prices up to a combined $1.01, with the pair executed at $1.00 beyond that. Search results say the fee schedule filing at https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03242641956.pdf sets $0.01 per contract per side, independent of netting; that PDF could not be parsed, so this is [S] and the existing "Commission zero at IBKR" row may be a different layer (IBKR commission vs exchange fee). Recommend tagging a new fee row `needs owner` or [S] until the schedule is read.
- Time zone (goal c): Rule 101(b)(4) (p9) says all references to time are Chicago local time unless stated otherwise, and Rule 101(a)(12) defines Business Day as Mon-Fri 8:00 CT to 17:00 CT excluding equity market holidays. Individual rules use both "CST" and "CT" (for example 16:00 CST in 604(a), 16:00 CT in 311(c)). No explicit DST statement exists, so the label "CST" is ambiguous; best reading is Chicago local time (CT, DST-observing).
- Redline: the Sept 3 2026 copy shows tracked changes (new Rule 418 Block Trades, minimum 25,000 contracts; Rule 417(c) typo fix). Rules 604, 612, 401 and 608 were not marked as changed in this copy.
- Rule 405(b): Affiliated Participant Bids are always filled last at every price level (affects any IBKR-affiliated flow).
