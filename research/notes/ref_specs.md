## Fee finding

- Source: CFTC self-certification dated 2026-03-02, "Modifications to ForecastEx Pairing Threshold", https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03022640215.pdf, p 1 (accessed 2026-10-03). [V]
- Effective 2026-03-31 at 4:15 PM CT (maintenance window 4:15 to 5:15 PM CT, all outstanding bids cancelled). Pairing moves from yes+no bids totalling $1.01 to $1.00. Before, the extra $0.01 collected at execution became ForecastEx fee income at resolution; now ForecastEx charges "a transaction fee of $0.01 per side per contract" (fee schedule is Exhibit B, not in the text copy). Volume Incentive Program and Market Maker Program rebased from settled to transacted volume and the MM incentive rates changed (Exhibits C and D, confidential).
- Current rulebook agrees on the $1.00 pairing: https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules09032623612.pdf Rule 403(d) "Inverse Pricing", p 30, and Rule 403(c), p 32 (aggressing bid price-improved so the pair equals $1.00). That rulebook text sets the pairing only; the per-side fee amount is in the Exhibit B fee schedule, which I could not read.
- Implication for venue_facts.md "Commission [S] zero commission at IBKR": ForecastEx's own fee is $0.01 per side per contract from 2026-03-31. Whether IBKR passes it through, waives it, or charges its own commission is [?] (see owner item 4). Per-trade cost is 1 cent against a $0.01 tick, which matters for quoting edge.
