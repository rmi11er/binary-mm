## Notes
- All three CME-futures-referenced index contracts share: Source Agency = CME; settlement price taken from the CME daily bulletin; resolution 10:00 AM CT when CME publishes the final bulletin; settlement 1:00 PM CT same day if resolution is before 12:00 PM CT else next business day; not subject to early resolution. Source: https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/orgrules/25/05/rules05262522278.pdf (accessed 2026-10-03).
- Last trading time for FES, FNQ, FYM amended from 2:30 PM CT to 3:00 PM CT, effective 2025-06-09: https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/orgrules/25/05/rules05262522277.pdf (accessed 2026-10-03).
- Side finding (affects venue_facts.md "Commission [S] zero commission" row): a CFTC filing dated 2026-03-02 moves the pairing threshold from $1.01 to $1.00 and introduces a transaction fee of $0.01 per side per contract, effective 2026-03-31 4:15 PM CT, and shifts the Volume Incentive and Market Maker programs from settlement-based to transaction-based. https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03022640215.pdf (accessed 2026-10-03). Needs reconciliation with Task 5/fees owner; I did not verify whether the fee is passed through or waived at IBKR.
